ACCOUNT OWNERSHIP AND DELETION — PROPOSAL FOR VICKI
Not built, activated or promised to customers. Requires approval before implementation.

THE SIMPLE EXPERIENCE
A person opens Account & Privacy in Waist Time, chooses View my information,
Correct an entry, Get a copy, or Delete my account. Editing uses existing entry
controls. Export/account deletion need new supported tools. Vicki never writes SQL
or reviews private health entries to establish who owns an account.

VERIFY OWNERSHIP
1. For sensitive requests, ask the signed-in person to confirm with a fresh code
   sent to the email already attached to their account. They enter it inside the
   trusted app; they never forward it to Vicki or put it into a support email.
2. The server verifies the code with Supabase and checks the returned stable user
   ID equals the account being acted on. Do not trust a typed email, screenshot,
   email sender, client-side user ID or an old refreshed session as proof of a new
   deletion request. Do not let an arbitrary replacement email become the target.
3. A new server-side verification service must issue a short-lived, single-use
   authorization tied to that user, the specific request and its intended action.
   Rate-limit code/request attempts, prevent reuse and cross-account substitution,
   and keep tokens/codes out of logs. A general sign-in OTP does not by itself
   prove consent to deletion; require a separate clear final confirmation.
4. If mailbox access is lost, pause destructive work. A separately approved account
   recovery process is needed; Support must not bypass verification using health
   facts, an email address or screenshots. This flow is not available today.

WHAT VICKI DOES
Receive a request through the confirmed support mailbox. Acknowledge it and point
people to their own in-app controls. In the proposed private Support Desk, see only
request ID, verified/not-verified state, action requested, dates and outcome—not
health entries or photographs. Provide export only through the verified user's
protected download, never to an unverified email destination. Correction is normally
done by the person in their app. Never ask for their code or claim completion before
an automated job and its checks actually succeed. The desk/export process must be
built; until then requests can be logged privately but not promised fulfillment.

SAFE ACCOUNT DELETION TO BUILD
Explain exactly what will be removed and let the person preserve wanted information
first. Address unsynced entries and device-local photos explicitly. A separate
confirmation follows fresh ownership verification; signing out/uninstalling is not
this confirmation. Avoid unnecessary obstacles or support-only completion.

A trusted backend job first blocks new writes and replays for that account, then
removes its health records and associated conflicts/receipts/change content, handles
approved residual/legal records, revokes sessions and removes the Auth account in
a safe dependency order. Use retry-safe stages and an audited outcome; never put
privileged credentials in the app. Auth deletion alone and individual-entry purge
are not complete solutions. Existing access tokens and stale devices must be denied
by server-side account state even before token expiry. Test partial failures.

On the initiating device, clear only this account's local records/queued changes
and saved credential after the confirmed process. The exact local photo handling
needs Vicki's decision and a clear user warning; originals in Photos/Files must not
be removed. Other connected devices need scoped cleanup when they learn the result.
Disconnected or lost devices cannot be remotely guaranteed erased. The app must not
allow old pending writes to recreate the deleted account or records after reconnect.

Backups are separate: establish actual retention and a process that reapplies
completed deletion decisions if restoring a backup. Keep only minimal approved
verification/audit records for an explicitly justified period, not private entry
content. Do not promise immediate removal from every historic backup or OS backup.

CONFIRMATION
Show a private completion receipt and send a minimal confirmation to the verified
account contact only after successful checks. No health details. Say what was
removed, any justified retained information and known offline-device/backup limits.
If a stage fails, show pending/needs attention rather than “deleted.” Vicki can see
that status without reading the user's records. Response times and retention periods
must be set before public promises. This proposal does not authorize any deletion.
